xlamBOT 1.2 for Android

A free Android APK that recognizes the visible Brawl Stars screen and controls the game using touch gestures. The new dark/lime control panel includes consistent tabs, animated level selectors, a tile-based brawler picker, queues, statistics and Plus.

Free

  • Movement, attacks and automatic abilities.
  • Cautious, Universal and Aggressive play styles.
  • Normal Autododge and gas avoidance.
  • Brawler queues and rotation after matches or net +N trophies.
  • Statistics, recovery, compact overlay and stop controls.

Optional Plus

50 Telegram Stars for 30 days, renewed manually. Enhanced Autododge, predictive AutoAim, bush mode, power boxes and cubes, fast reaction, supported GPU/NNAPI acceleration and occasional pins. NPU assignment depends on the device driver. Background gameplay and lock-screen operation are not included.

Trials have been removed with 1.2; existing paid, VIP and referral access is preserved. Every five new referred users who enter the Telegram bot and confirm channel membership earn the inviter ten additional Plus days. Channel membership gates Telegram/Mini App access, not Android gameplay.

Get started

  1. Download the APK and install it over the existing app.
  2. Enable xlamBOT in Accessibility and connect the image source.
  3. Use English in Brawl Stars, standard controls and Trio Showdown for the first test.
  4. Choose the matching brawler and start. After an update, toggle the accessibility service off and on.

Requirements and support

Android 8+, ARM64 / ARMv7 / x86_64. Android 11+ can use accessibility screenshots; Android 8–10 needs system screen capture. Hardware acceleration and app-only capture depend on the device. Detailed Russian manual → · Plus and referrals →.
